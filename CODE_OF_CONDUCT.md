# Code of Conduct

Sue is made by people who draw, write, compose, film and code. Treat each
other the way you would want to be treated in a shared studio.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique work, never people.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone starts somewhere, and questions are welcome.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or unwanted attention, here or in
  any space where you represent the project.
- Respect other people's work: credit what you use, and do not submit code
  or art you have no right to share.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these
rules, and may block people who keep breaking them. To report a problem,
contact the maintainers privately through GitHub.
